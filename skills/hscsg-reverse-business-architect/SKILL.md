# HSCSG Reverse Business Architect Skill

## Purpose
Reverse-engineer iconic company business models and map them to new niches with launchable offers, X-for-Y hooks, and 30-day execution plans.

## When to Use
- Analyzing a successful company to extract its mechanics
- Applying proven models to new niches
- Creating launchable offers with multiple frameworks
- Generating X-for-Y positioning hooks
- Building 30-day execution plans

## Inputs Required
1. Company/brand name to model after
2. Target niche/audience
3. Optional custom offer style/framework

## Methodology (from Arquitecto de Negocios de Construcción Inversa + HSCSG v15 OS)

### Phase 1: Narrative Reverse-Engineering (Perspectiva Ontológica)
- Full narrative analysis of company business model (qué ES)
- Revenue model, delivery format, growth strategies
- Differentiators, cultural/brand factors
- Written as founder-style case study
- **Alráico mapping**: Identificar PI topológico, 20 límites cognitivos, 𝕮 (credeófilos αʰ=Ω·s, γ, κ)

### Phase 2: Business Mechanics Playbook (Perspectiva Teleológica) — 3-5 playbooks
- Why it works, what problem it solves, cross-industry reuse
- **γ-CARMIS trigger**: ΣPᵢ > κ → reconfiguración consciente del modelo
- **ECROx mapping**: αʰ (armonía), s (sincronía), y (ligadura), v (rigidez), XP (carga), k (umbral)

### Phase 3: Strategic Narrative for User's Niche (Perspectiva Axiológica) — valorar
- Identify audience, paint challenges, show remixed solution
- Investor-pitch style narrative
- **AUT/CDS Gate MJ**: Verificar coeficiente de autonomía ≥ 0.618 antes de lanzar
- **Resonancia check**: αʰ₁·αʰ₂·3.0 > αʰ₁+αʰ₂ entre modelo origen y nicho destino

### Phase 4: 10 X-for-Y Hooks + **Alráico Transduction Hooks**
- Distinct, emotional/curiosity-driven, headline-ready
- **Transducción F**: {modelo_origen} → 𝕮 → {nicho_destino} (triaxial verification)

### Phase 5: Launchable Offers (6 total) + **Alráico Offer Variants**
1. Custom style (if provided)
2. Hormozi-Style Offer → **Hormozi-Alráico** (γ-CARMIS pricing)
3. Dan Kennedy Offer → **Kennedy-Alráico** (direct response + ECROx)
4. Brunson Funnel Offer → **Brunson-Alráico** (funnel resonante αʰ)
5. Indie Hacker MVP → **Indie-Alráico** (AUT ≥ 0.618, CDS bajo)
6. Productized Service → **Service-Alráico** (servicio productizado anfibio)

Each offer includes: Name, Framework, Description, Audience Logic, Pricing Model, Delivery Method, 30-Day Execution Plan (coaching-style)
- **30-day plan**: Tick Alráico (6 loops + γ-CARMIS + resonancia) semanal
- **Gate MJ**: Verificar AUT ≥ 0.618 antes de cada fase

### Phase 6: Bonus Angle + **Alráico Lead Magnet**
- Lead magnet, waitlist strategy, or pivot concept
- **Alráico Lead Magnet**: Canvas Alráico personalizado (20 límites + ECROx)

### Anfibio Mode
- **Offline (RAO local)**: Ofertas operan en modo postmonetario (ZNU/CaaS)
- **Conectado (Nostr/NEAR/HTTP)**: Ofertas operan con precio USDC vía oráculo priceParity
- **Render decide etiqueta**, lógica agnóstica a unidad

## Output Format
- Company Reverse-Engineered (founder-style case study)
- Core Business Mechanics Playbook (3-5 mechanics)
- Applied to Niche: Strategic Narrative (pitch deck meets blog post)
- 10 X-for-Y Hooks
- 6 Launchable Offers with full details
- Bonus Angle/Lead Magnet

## Constraints
- Narrative-first, bullet points only for clarity
- Founder-style prose, not product specs
- 30-day plans as coaching briefs, not step lists
- Minimum 10 X-for-Y hooks
- 6 offers minimum (custom + 5 standard)

## Example Usage
```
Company: Stripe
Niche: Freelance developers needing payment infrastructure
Custom Style: Indie Hacker
```
